# Pith: product

## Platform
web (single-file dashboard served from loopback; also used on phone/tablet over the LAN via an explicit token)

## What it is
Local-first second brain. Anything saved (links, reels, articles, PDFs, images, notes) becomes one
durable note that holds the actual information (steps, numbers, names) with a pointer back to the
source. Capture and consumption are separate: capture happens elsewhere (drop folder, HTTP share
endpoint, CLI, Telegram export, paste/drop on the page); the dashboard is where knowledge is read,
searched and reused.

## Users and use scene
One person, usually in the evening after a day of saving ~20 things, on a laptop or phone, reading.
Months later: retrieving one obscure detail. Thousands of sources over time.

## Purpose
Open Pith, see what arrived, read what matters, find any detail, trust what is shown, recover
anything that failed. No filing, no categorising, no pipeline vocabulary.

## Principles
- Reading is the centrepiece. Notes are read, not scanned as cards.
- The system organises (topics are automatic); the user may correct, edit, reprocess, delete.
- Honest about confidence: inferred / low-confidence / gaps are visible, never hidden.
- Failures are named in plain language with one-click recovery.
- Every screen justifies itself: one workspace (list + reader), one command surface (search + ask).

## Brand / tone
Quiet, literate, exact. A reading room, not a dashboard. Copy names actions and states plainly.

## Anti-references
Stat dashboards, card grids, chat-bot UIs, gradient AI aesthetics, Notion-style chrome.
