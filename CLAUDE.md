# Pith

## UI/UX work

Before any UI/UX task (dashboard in `frontend/`, design exploration, prototypes, critique, responsive work), read `docs/design/UI_UX_SKILL_PLAYBOOK.md`. It has the task-to-recipe routing table, which design skills to use or avoid, and the quality gates. Do not implement a new surface or redesign before its brief is approved; small fixes and audits follow their recipes directly. The playbook governs process only; it does not set IA or visual decisions.
